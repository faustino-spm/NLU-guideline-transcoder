### Disposition Block: RPC + PTP (`<SLUG>_RIGHT_PARTY_CONTACT` and `<SLUG>_PTP`)
Generate **TWO** dispositions in this exact order:
1. `<SLUG>_RIGHT_PARTY_CONTACT` (Right Party Contact without a finalized Promise to Pay)
2. `<SLUG>_PTP` (Right Party Contact with a Promise to Pay)

**Rules & Deviations:**
- **Full 1:1 Client-Rubric Mirror:** Include every scored criterion, every Zero Tolerance Policy (ZTP) / Compliance policy item, and any 3rd-Party Contact (3PC) section if explicitly printed on the form (marking 3PC items as `not_applicable` in both dispositions with empty `Component Score`).
